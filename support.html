ChocksLog: Pilot Logbook · flight time, duty & rest

Need help, found a problem, or want a logbook format or duty rule added? Email danielblanco@me.com. Please say which device you use and, for a problem, what you did and what you saw.

Getting started

Open the app and add a flight or simulator session, or import your logbook (Import accepts FLYLOG and other logbook PDFs, Excel and CSV). Set your totals brought forward under Starting totals. The built-in Help screen explains every feature.

Sync between iPhone, iPad and the website

Records sync through your own iCloud. Sign in to iCloud on your devices, and on the website (danielblanco-del.github.io/chocks) sign in with the same Apple ID. Pull the page down in the app to sync now. Settings → iCloud sync shows the last sync and lets you compare and repair.

Chocks Pro

The logbook is free. Chocks Pro (monthly or yearly subscription, first month free for new subscribers) adds duty & rest checks, roster, monthly duty record, printed logbook pages and reading limits from your operations manual. Restore it on a new device in Settings → Chocks Pro → Restore purchases. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings → Subscriptions.

Privacy

Your records stay on your device and in your iCloud; the developer collects nothing. See the Privacy Policy.
