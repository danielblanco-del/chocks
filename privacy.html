Chocks — Privacy Policy

ChocksLog: Pilot Logbook, for iPhone, iPad and the web · Last updated 17 November 2026

Chocks is a pilot logbook with flight duty and rest control, made by Daniel Blanco. In short: your records are yours. They are kept on your device and in your own iCloud account. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share them.

What Chocks stores, and where

  • On your device: your logbook entries, duty days, roster, aircraft and simulators, settings, signature and starting totals.
  • In your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit, private database): the same records, so they sync between your iPhone, iPad and the Chocks website. They are stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms; the developer has no access to them.
  • Documents you upload (an operations manual or regulation, logbook exports to import) are read on your device. Only the limits you confirm and the short passages they came from are saved with your settings. The document itself stays on the device you used.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Chocks has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not see your name, email, records or usage.

Sharing with your Operations

If you choose to give your company's Operations person access (Settings → Operations access), your records are shared with their Apple ID through iCloud sharing. They can see your duty and rest, monthly duty record and logbook, propose duties, publish your roster and your company's rules. You can remove their access at any time.

Subscriptions

Chocks Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the developer receives no payment details. Your subscription status is kept with your records in your iCloud so the website can unlock it too.

Website

The Chocks website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. Signing in uses Sign in with Apple through Apple's CloudKit JS.

Deleting your records

Settings → Clear records removes them from the app and your iCloud. You can also delete the app's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

Chocks is a professional tool for pilots and is not directed at children.
